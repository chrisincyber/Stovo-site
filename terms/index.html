The app

Stovo is published by Petertil TripPortier. You use the app under Apple’s Standard License Agreement, which is also the Terms of Use linked in the app. How Stovo handles data is described in the Privacy Policy.

Product information is general information

Product details, scores, diet and label information, and additive notes are for general information only. They are not an official product label or a guarantee, and may be incomplete, inaccurate or out of date. Always check the package for current information, especially for allergies, intolerances or dietary needs. Stovo does not provide medical or personal dietary advice.

Stovo marks where product facts come from. “Community submitted” and “Unverified community information” mean that one Stovo user typed the facts from a package. “Community confirmed” means that at least two other app identities, different from the one that added the fact, entered or confirmed the same value for the same product and package market. That does not prove they are different people, and it is not an official check. Facts from Open Food Facts are shown as supplied by Open Food Facts, with the date they were last updated there; Stovo has not checked them.

Adding product facts

When you tap Add on the form for a product’s facts, what you entered is shared with Stovo’s catalogue, and the form says so. Scanning a product does not share anything. Please add only what you read on the package, as facts, and do not submit links, advertising, or anything about other people.

You keep whatever rights you have in what you add. You give Stovo a non-exclusive, free permission to store it in the shared catalogue, show it to other Stovo users with the package market and a community label, combine it with other submissions for the same product, and correct, hold back or remove it, for as long as it stays in the catalogue. Stovo does not use it for anything else. Stovo may hold back, change or remove product facts, for example when two submissions disagree or when a fact is reported as wrong. You cannot edit or delete a submission yourself, and after a short time Stovo can no longer tell which facts came from you; for a question about a product’s facts, use Report a problem in the app or write to info@stovo.ch. What is sent and how long it is kept is described in the Privacy Policy.

Open Food Facts

When Stovo’s catalogue has no entry, Stovo can show data from Open Food Facts, a community database available under the Open Database License. It is shown with its source, is not copied into Stovo’s catalogue, and can be corrected at Open Food Facts.

Your account

An account is optional. If you sign in, you are responsible for what happens under your sign-in and for keeping access to your Apple Account or email address. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Account); how your data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

AI features

Stovo AI uses Apple’s on-device model and, for recipes, ideas and open conversation, ChatGPT from OpenAI, as described in the privacy policy. Where it is on, Stovo does not ask before each message; you can turn ChatGPT off in Settings. Some AI use is free up to a small number of messages and then needs Stovo Premium. What the AI says can be wrong; you check it before anything is saved.

Recipe suggestions

Recipes written by the assistant or suggested by Stovo AI are not tested. They can be wrong or unsafe. Check cooking times, temperatures and ingredients yourself. They are not medical, dietary or allergy advice.
