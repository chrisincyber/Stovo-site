iCloud sync

How do I turn on iCloud sync?

Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app on each device. Stovo then syncs your lists and pantry on its own. You can check the status in Stovo under Settings → iCloud & Sync.

Does Stovo work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection. Changes made offline sync the next time the device is online.

Sync says “Needs Attention” or “Paused”.

“Needs Attention” usually means your iCloud storage is full; Stovo keeps working on the device and syncs again once there is space. “Paused” means a different iCloud account is now signed in; Stovo keeps your data on the device until you choose to sync it with the new account.

Sharing a household

How do I share my lists and pantry?

With iCloud sync on, open Settings → Household → Share Household and invite people. Everyone needs an Apple Account. The people you invite see and edit the same lists, pantry and history.

How do I accept an invitation?

Open the invitation link on an iPhone or iPad that has Stovo installed and is signed in to iCloud. Stovo opens the shared household; your own household stays too, and you can switch between them in Settings → Household.

How do I leave a household or stop sharing?

If you were invited, open Settings → Household and choose Leave Household; the shared data is removed from your devices and the others keep it. If you own the household, choose Manage Sharing to remove people or stop sharing; you keep everything.

A new device, or restoring

I have a new iPhone or iPad. How do I get my data?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and install Stovo. With iCloud sync, your lists and pantry arrive automatically.

I don’t use iCloud.

Then your data lives only on the device it was entered on. Restoring an iPhone or iPad backup restores it; otherwise it cannot be moved to another device.

Something is missing on one device.

Make sure both devices are online and signed in to the same iCloud account, then open Stovo on both. Changes usually arrive within a few moments once both are connected.

Stovo shows a screen saying it can’t open its data.

Your data is not deleted. Tap Try Again first. If it keeps happening, tap Copy Details for Support and send them to us — they help us find the cause.

Pantry

How does shopping reach the pantry?

When you check an item off your shopping list, it’s added to your pantry automatically. Checked off the wrong item? Tap it under Last Used within two minutes to undo the purchase.

How do I add things I already have?

In Pantry, tap “Add to pantry…” and type what you have, for example “500 g Rice” or “2 Milk”. If the product is already in your pantry, the amount is added to it.

How do I correct an amount?

Swipe a pantry item to take away a small amount, or open it and choose Adjust Stock to remove or add an exact amount, or Set to Zero. The oldest stock is used up first. Corrections never change your shopping history.

What are Fridge, Pantry and Other?

Every pantry item is kept in one place — Fridge, Pantry or Other (in German: Kühlschrank, Vorrat, Sonstiges). Stovo picks a sensible place for common products; to change it, open the item and choose a Location, or touch and hold it and choose Move to. Stovo remembers your choice for next time. The filter at the top of Pantry shows one place at a time.

Languages

Which languages does Stovo support?

English and German. Stovo follows your device language. To use a different language only for Stovo, open Settings → Language in Stovo, which takes you to Stovo’s page in the Settings app. Products are recognized in both languages, so “Milch” and “Milk” land in the same place.

Requirements

Stovo 1.0 for iPhone and iPad, with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. Available in English and German.
