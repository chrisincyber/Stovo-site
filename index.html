Stovo

Know what you have. Know what you need.

A shopping list and a pantry that keep each other up to date. Check something off while you shop, and it’s at home in your pantry.

Coming soonon the App Store for iPhone and iPad

Smart shopping lists

Type “2 Milk” or “500 g Chicken” and it’s on the list, sorted into the right category automatically. Two lists are free; Stovo Premium has unlimited lists.

Shopping flows into the pantry

Check something off and it’s in your pantry. Tapped the wrong row? Undo it within two minutes. Last Used brings it back in one tap.

Fridge and pantry

See what’s in the fridge and what’s in the cupboard. Add what you already have, and correct amounts as you use them.

Shared household

Invite the people you live with. Everyone sees and edits the same lists and pantry. Or share just one list — for a party, a trip or the office. Free.

iCloud sync, offline first

Everything works without a connection and stays up to date on your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud.

Gentle reminders

A weekly shopping reminder, or a reminder for a single list — only when there’s something left to buy. Optional notes when someone joins or leaves your household. Never for every edit. Free.

Siri & Shortcuts

“Add 2 Milk to my Stovo list.” Add items, open a list or check what’s at home by voice or from Shortcuts. Part of Stovo Premium; you switch it on in Settings.

English and German

“Milch” or “Milk” — Stovo recognizes common products in both languages.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking, and no Stovo server for your lists. Your data lives on your devices and in your own iCloud. Privacy Policy
